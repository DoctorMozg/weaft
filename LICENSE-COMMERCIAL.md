## Who needs a commercial license

The AGPL-3.0 is a strong copyleft license. Among other obligations, it requires
any party that conveys a modified version of weaft, or offers weaft's
functionality to third parties over a network, to make the corresponding
source code of that version available to those users under the AGPL-3.0.

You likely need a commercial license if you want to:

- **Embed or redistribute weaft** inside a proprietary product, appliance,
  container image, SDK, or virtual machine image that you ship to customers,
  without making the surrounding product available under the AGPL-3.0.
- **Offer weaft as a hosted or managed service** to third parties without
  publishing the corresponding source code under the AGPL-3.0.
- **Modify weaft** and distribute those modifications to customers without
  releasing the modifications under the AGPL-3.0.

You do **not** need a commercial license if you use weaft unmodified inside
your own organization (including on self-hosted CI), or if you are prepared
to meet the AGPL-3.0's source-disclosure obligations for the scenarios above.

## What the commercial license provides

A commercial license replaces the AGPL-3.0 obligations for the paying
customer and, at a minimum, grants:

1. **Redistribution rights.** A non-exclusive, non-transferable right to
   include and redistribute weaft — in source, binary, or container form —
   as part of your own product, service, or deployment, without triggering
   the AGPL-3.0 share-alike requirements for your surrounding code.

2. **Support.** A direct support channel with the maintainer, scoped to
   weaft itself (its CLI, the skill and subagent compiler, the host
   capability matrix, and the target backends). Response-time targets,
   support hours, and incident caps are set in the purchase agreement.

The commercial license does **not** cover:

- Warranty beyond what the purchase agreement explicitly states.
